# The Crew mechanical data

`deep_sea_tasks.json` contains the 96 task conditions and their three-, four-,
and five-seat difficulty ratings. Text labels and the evaluator are original.
No card artwork or narrative text is included.

Rules and mission conditions were checked against the publisher's manuals:

- https://www.thamesandkosmos.com/manuals/full/691868_Crew_Manual.pdf
- https://www.thamesandkosmos.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/691869_Crew_Deep-Sea_Manual.pdf
- https://c.tabletopia.com/games/mission-deep-sea/rules/logbook/en
- https://www.64ouncegames.com/pages/the-crew-log-book

The numerical task inventory was cross-checked against the public mechanical
catalog at https://github.com/FredeAlexandre/crew/blob/main/packages/engine/src/tasks/catalog.ts
and https://pdfcoffee.com/the-crew-deep-sea-mission-cards-pdf-free.html.
The publisher's card examples take precedence: sum thresholds are inclusive,
sum tricks exclude submarines, and green 3 / yellow 4 / yellow 5 are one task.

Online conventions: the official untimed alternatives are used for Deep Sea
missions 14–16 and 26. In two-player exchanges the commander selects from the
helper's face-up cards. Task failure retries reshuffle both hands and tasks.
